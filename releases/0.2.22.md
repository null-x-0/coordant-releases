# Coordant 0.2.22

Conversations now have persistent task lists in the right sidebar. Agents can create and update their own steps through Coordant, and you can add, check off, or remove tasks. Lists retain their progress across stop, resume, and archive. Revision checks protect concurrent edits.

Session costs now use the same streaming usage reader as Models and costs, so large Codex transcripts retain their model prices. Estimates follow your editable rates. A small dot separates tokens from cost in the bottom bar.

The message navigator shows up to ten markers, with mouse-wheel scrolling and arrows that disappear at each end. Newly visible markers fade in when motion is enabled. Keyboard navigation and short split panes remain supported.

The Models and costs introduction now aligns with the Account limits heading. The new task controls follow the selected theme.

Verified with disposable conversation data, authenticated agent-tool calls, task retention and deletion checks, large-transcript pricing, native provider checks, and macOS packaging tests.
