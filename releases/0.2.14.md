Coordant 0.2.14 adds Obsidian, a near-black theme with neutral grey panels and soft silver text.

Choose it in Settings → Appearance. The palette applies throughout the app and open terminals, bringing the total to nineteen. Your current selection stays selected, and Coordant Dark remains the default.
