Coordant 0.2.13 adds more color palettes and tidies the end of Settings.

- Coordant Light pairs a white canvas with soft grey panels. The original palette is now labeled Coordant Dark and remains the default. Your selected palette stays selected.
- Six more palettes bring the total to eighteen: Linen, Mist, Sage, Dusk, Ember, and Cobalt. Each applies throughout the app and open terminals. Alternative palettes keep text and status colors readable on both canvas and panel backgrounds.
- Settings keeps dividers between preferences without an extra line beneath the last row. Palette choices retain their compact layout, including the final unpaired option.
