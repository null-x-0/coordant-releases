Coordant 0.2.9 adds selectable palettes, regional preferences, and smoother chat delivery.

- Choose ten new palettes alongside the original Coordant colors in Settings → Appearance. The selected palette keeps the full swatch display; alternatives use compact rows. Changes apply immediately throughout the app, including open terminals. Eight palettes adapt open-source themes with licenses included.
- Set your time zone and regional format, or keep automatic detection. Chat, usage, archives, notifications, updates, and schedules use these preferences. Calendar schedules keep their clock time when you change zones.
- Messages appear as normal chat bubbles immediately after sending. Failed or unconfirmed deliveries show clear recovery actions. Cursor messages hide their timestamp and user-query wrappers, using the native timestamp where available.
- New messages without native timestamps retain the time Coordant first receives them. These fallback times survive reopening; old imported history is not assigned a made-up arrival time.
- Delete extra accounts from Settings with confirmation. Their sessions stop, model profiles are removed, and affected schedules pause. Saved chats, CLI sign-ins, and project files remain on disk.
- Pull repository changes from the Review panel. Pulling fast-forwards a clean branch from its configured upstream and explains when local changes or diverged commits need attention.
- The right sidebar’s tabs scroll with a normal mouse wheel. The continuation dialog has consistent form spacing, and failed-message buttons follow the shared button style.
